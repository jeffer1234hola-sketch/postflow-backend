Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Introduction

PostFlow ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website postflow.club. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our social media scheduling platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and password when you create an account. We also collect content you upload (images, videos, captions) for scheduling purposes.

When you connect a social media account (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), we receive an access token that allows us to publish content on your behalf. We do not store your social media passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve PostFlow's services, including scheduling and publishing your content to connected social media platforms, generating AI-powered captions, and displaying analytics about your posts.

4. TikTok Integration

PostFlow integrates with TikTok's Content Posting API. When you connect your TikTok account, we request permission to publish content on your behalf. We only post content that you explicitly schedule through PostFlow. We do not access your TikTok followers, messages, or other private data beyond what is necessary for posting.

Your TikTok access tokens are stored securely and never shared with third parties. You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time from your PostFlow settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Media files are stored via ImageKit CDN. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your information.

6. Third-Party Services

PostFlow uses the following third-party services: MongoDB Atlas (database), ImageKit (media storage), Groq (AI caption generation), Railway (server hosting), and Netlify (web hosting). Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at jeffer@postflow.club.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the PostFlow platform.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at jeffer@postflow.club.
